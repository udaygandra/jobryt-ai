EVIDENCE-FIRST EVALUATION
Before assigning scores, identify the evidence in the posting and compare it to the candidate context.

Score each pillar from 0-100:
- skills_fit_score: compare the posting's requirements to the candidate's demonstrated skills.
  * SPECIAL EDGE CASE: If the job description does not mention specific technical skills, tools, or qualification bullet points (e.g. short recruiter note, agency summary, or roles where technical skills are discussed over the phone/call), assign skills_fit_score = 90. In `notes_concerns`, write: "Skills not specified in posting — 90% default applied for recruiter call screening."
  * Otherwise, compare required skills against candidate skills. Required skills matter more than nice-to-haves.
- seniority_fit_score: compare the posting's required years/level and title seniority to the candidate's years and target roles. A gap of 3+ years either way scores below 50.
  * If the posting is for Lead, Principal, Staff, Manager, Director, VP, or Head, but the candidate does NOT have those in their target roles, score seniority below 40.
  * If the candidate targets Executive/Management roles, entry-level/internship roles must score below 30.
- location_fit_score: compare the posting location and workplace type (Remote/Hybrid/Onsite) to candidate's locations and commute radius ({{COMMUTE_RADIUS_KM}} km).
  * 100% Genuine Nationwide Remote roles (when candidate accepts Remote): 100% (zero travel required; includes "City (Remote)", "Remote - Canada", and nationwide remote).
  * CRITICAL: Hybrid in another city (e.g. Vancouver, Calgary, Ottawa for a Toronto candidate) is NOT remote. It requires physical attendance in that distant city. If a job is Hybrid or On-site outside the candidate's commute radius, score <= 20% and add to `disqualification_reasons`: "Job location is outside candidate commuting area and is not a 100% remote nationwide role."
  * Onsite / Hybrid roles: evaluate physical distance from candidate's location against radius R ({{COMMUTE_RADIUS_KM}} km):
    - Within 20% of radius (distance <= 0.20 * R, e.g. <= 5 km for 25km, <= 10 km for 50km): score in 90-100% gradient.
    - Within 50% of radius (distance <= 0.50 * R, e.g. <= 15 km for 25km, <= 25 km for 50km): score in 80-90% gradient.
    - Within 100% of radius (distance <= 1.00 * R, e.g. <= 25 km for 25km, <= 50 km for 50km): score in 70-80% gradient.
    - Beyond radius (distance > R): score <= 20% (hard location blocker; add to disqualification_reasons).
  * Candidate requested Remote-only but role is strictly Onsite/Hybrid: score 0-15 (add to disqualification_reasons).
- title_fit_score: judge by the actual duties/function, not title wording alone.
  90-100 = target role or senior/junior variation.
  70-89 = different title but substantially the same function.
  40-69 = adjacent function with meaningful overlap.
  0-39 = different profession/function.
