EVIDENCE-FIRST EVALUATION
Before assigning scores, identify the evidence in the posting and compare it to the candidate context.

Score each pillar from 0-100:
- skills_fit_score: compare the posting's requirements to the candidate's demonstrated skills.
  * SPECIAL EDGE CASE: If the job description does not mention specific technical skills, tools, or qualification bullet points (e.g. short recruiter note, agency summary, or roles where technical skills are discussed over the phone/call), assign skills_fit_score = 70. In `notes_concerns`, write: "Skills not specified in posting — 70% default applied for recruiter call screening."
  * Otherwise, compare required skills against candidate skills. Required skills matter more than nice-to-haves. A candidate who only matches 3-4 skills out of 20-30 stated requirements should be scored around 50-60% on skills fit. Do not inflate skills fit above 75% unless the candidate possesses the majority of primary required tools.
- seniority_fit_score: compare the posting's required years/level and title seniority to the candidate's years and target roles. A gap of 3+ years either way scores below 50.
  * If the posting is for Lead, Principal, Staff, Manager, Director, VP, or Head, but the candidate does NOT have those in their target roles, score seniority below 40.
  * If the candidate targets Executive/Management roles, entry-level/internship roles must score below 30.
- function_fit_score: judge by actual duties and role function, not surface title phrasing alone.
  90-100 = target role or senior/junior variation.
  70-89 = different title but substantially the same function.
  40-69 = adjacent function with meaningful overlap.
  0-39 = different profession/function.
- NOTE ON LOCATION: Location fit is computed 100% deterministically by the system from the candidate's commute radius ({{COMMUTE_RADIUS_KM}} km) and GPS coordinates. Do NOT score location; focus your evaluation on skills, seniority, and functional role match.
