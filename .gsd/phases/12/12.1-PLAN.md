# Plan 12.1: Country-First Hierarchical Location Selector & Scoped City Combobox

## Objective
Re-architect the Settings page location workflow into a clean hierarchical pattern: place Target Country as the primary selector, keep City input disabled until a country is selected, and restrict city search results strictly to the cities belonging to the selected country (with country-specific remote/hybrid options).

## Context
- [index.html](file:///c:/Users/udays/Documents/jobryt-ai/public/index.html)
- [settings.js](file:///c:/Users/udays/Documents/jobryt-ai/public/js/settings.js)
- [global-cities.json](file:///c:/Users/udays/Documents/jobryt-ai/public/data/global-cities.json)
