# Plan 28.2: Pure Settings-Driven Job Scan & Pipeline Execution

## Objective
Ensure the job search and evaluation pipeline (`src/routes/pipeline.js` and `scripts/engine/fetch-all-boards.js`) solely uses the criteria from Search Settings (`settings.target_titles` / `settings.target_roles` and `settings.locations`) for scanning live job boards and scoring opportunities, treating Master Profile strictly as candidate credentials (skills, experience, education) rather than search preferences.

## Context
- `src/routes/pipeline.js` (`executePipelineScan`)
- `scripts/engine/fetch-all-boards.js` (`fetchAllJobs`, `setSearchContext`)
- `scripts/core/settings-helper.js` (`getSettings`)

## Tasks

<task type="auto">
  <name>Enforce Settings Exclusivity in Pipeline Scan Execution</name>
  <files>src/routes/pipeline.js</files>
  <action>
    - In `src/routes/pipeline.js` (`executePipelineScan`):
      * Read target titles strictly from `overrides.targetTitles || settings.target_titles || settings.target_roles`.
      * Only fall back to `[profile.role]` if settings has 0 target roles configured.
      * Read target locations strictly from `overrides.targetLocations || settings.locations || settings.target_locations`.
      * Only fall back to `[profile.contact?.location || 'Remote']` if settings has 0 locations configured.
      * In `runGeoFilter`, supply the settings-derived `targetTitles` and `targetLocations`.
      * In `buildDynamicScoringPrompt`, pass the candidate's verified skills, experience, and education from `profile`, but strictly pass `targetTitles` and `targetLocations` from settings.
  </action>
  <verify>node -e "const p = require('./src/routes/pipeline'); console.log(typeof p.executePipelineScan)"</verify>
  <done>Pipeline solely relies on Search Settings preferences for job scanning and geo-filtering.</done>
</task>
