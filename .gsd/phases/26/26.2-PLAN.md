# Plan 26.2: Dynamic Universal Market Role Discovery for Any Global Skillset

## Objective
Fix the single-role bottleneck where uploading a "Full Stack Developer" (or any other discipline) only extracts 1 title. Enhance the LLM parsing prompt and the deterministic market role discovery engine to automatically generate 4 to 10 market-validated equivalent job titles for ANY skillset worldwide.

## Context
- prompts/resume-parser.txt
- scripts/engine/resume-to-profile.js
- scripts/engine/market-role-discovery.js
- scripts/engine/local-resume-extractor.js

## Tasks
