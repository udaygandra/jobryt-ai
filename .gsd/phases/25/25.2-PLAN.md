# Plan 25.2: 4-Expert Title Generation (Prompt B) & Parallel Market Demand Probing

## Objective
Implement Stage 3 and Stage 4 of the role discovery pipeline: use a 4-expert reasoning panel (Prompt B) to propose 15 candidate job titles across `core`, `adjacent`, and `stretch` tiers with resume evidence and synonyms, then probe live job board APIs in parallel to validate real employer demand and title match fidelity.

<task type="auto">
  <name>Author Prompt B template with 4-expert reasoning panel</name>
  <files>prompts/job-title-generation.txt</files>
  <action>
    - Ensure `prompts/job-title-generation.txt` (Prompt B):
      - Embeds 4-expert reasoning panel: Senior Technical Recruiter in candidate city, Principal Engineer, Product Owner / Systems Analyst, and Hiring Manager.
      - Enforces the 10 Hard Rules:
        1. Real posted titles only (exact phrasing employers actually post).
        2. No seniority inflation (calibrated to `current_level`).
        3. No duplicates by synonym (e.g. "BI Analyst" and "Business Intelligence Analyst" consolidated into one entry with synonyms).
        4. Evidence required (2-4 profile facts cited per title).
        5. Honest gaps (likely missing skills listed).
        6. Location-aware (regional conventions, dominant local employer clusters).
        7. Diversity across tiers: `core` (3-4), `adjacent` (3-4), `stretch` (1-2).
        8. Exactly 15 candidate titles output.
        9. No protected attributes.
        10. Prompt injection defense.
      - Output schema: `market_notes` and 15 `candidates` with `title`, `synonyms`, `tier`, `seniority_target`, `fit_score`, `evidence`, `likely_requirements_missing`, `must_have_keywords`, `exclude_keywords`, `target_industries`, `why_this_location`.
  </action>
