# Plan 25.3: Market-Accurate Role Selection (Up to 10 Roles) & Settings Synchronization

## Objective
Implement Stage 5: evaluate probed candidate titles using the composite demand-fit ranking formula, prune thin demand and overlapping titles, select up to 10 accurate roles (strictly quality-gated: only titles with verified market demand; do NOT pad to 10 if fewer meet the threshold), and automatically synchronize them into `settings.target_roles` and `profile.target_titles` when the user uploads their resume in "My Resume and Profile".

- src/routes/settings.js

- data/config/settings.json

## Tasks

<task type="auto">
  <name>Implement composite market-demand ranking and non-padding selection logic</name>
  <files>scripts/engine/market-validator.js</files>
  <action>
    - Ensure `rankAndFilterTitles(candidates, candidateLevel, options)` in `scripts/engine/market-validator.js`:
      - Applies hard demand gates:
        - Drops or demotes titles with `total_results < 15` (thin demand).
        - Drops titles with `title_match_rate < 0.5` (noisy query).
      - Deduplicates titles with $> 70\%$ token overlap, preserving the higher fit score and merging synonyms.
      - Calculates composite ranking score:
        $$\text{score} = 0.50 \times \text{fit\_score\_norm} + 0.20 \times \frac{\log_{10}(\text{total\_results}+1)}{\log_{10}(\text{max\_results}+1)} + 0.15 \times \text{seniority\_match} + 0.10 \times \text{title\_match\_rate} + 0.05 \times \text{recency\_score}$$
      - Quality-gated selection:
        - Selects UP TO 10 roles maximum.
        - Strict non-padding rule: only titles with composite score $\ge 60$ and verified market demand are selected (e.g. if only 4 or 6 roles meet criteria, return only those; never invent or pad titles to reach 10).
        - Enforces tier diversity: guarantees Core titles, at least 1 Adjacent, and at most 2 Stretch titles.
  </action>
  <verify>node -e "const { rankAndFilterTitles, computeCompositeScore } = require('./scripts/engine/market-validator'); console.log(typeof rankAndFilterTitles, typeof computeCompositeScore);"</verify>
  <done>Composite formula ranks probed titles and selects up to 10 accurate market titles without artificial padding.</done>
</task>
