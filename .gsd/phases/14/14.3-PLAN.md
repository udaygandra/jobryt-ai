# Plan 14.3: 10 Job Roles Guardrail & Slot Counter

## Objective
Enforce a hard limit of 10 Job Roles across the Settings page, Setup Wizard, and backend settings storage, accompanied by an informative slot counter `(X/10)`.

## Context
- `public/js/settings.js`
- `public/js/guide.js`
- `src/routes/settings.js`
- `public/index.html`

## Tasks

<task type="auto">
  <name>enforce-10-roles-limit</name>
  <files>public/js/settings.js, public/js/guide.js, src/routes/settings.js</files>
  <action>
    - In `public/js/settings.js`:
      - Set `MAX_ROLES = 10`.
      - In `addRoleTag()`: If `window.targetRolesList.length >= MAX_ROLES`, show toast warning `"Maximum 10 job roles allowed to maintain optimal search relevance."` and return early.
      - In `renderRoleTags()`: Update roles label or counter badge in UI to show `(${window.targetRolesList.length}/10)`. If 10 reached, update input placeholder.
    - In `public/js/guide.js`:
      - In `addWizardRole()`: Enforce `MAX_ROLES = 10`.
    - In `src/routes/settings.js`:
      - In `POST /api/settings`: Slice `target_titles` to max 10 before saving to settings and master profile.
  </action>
  <verify>grep -n "MAX_ROLES" public/js/settings.js</verify>
  <done>User is restricted to at most 10 target roles with live slot counter in UI.</done>
</task>

## Success Criteria
- [ ] Maximum 10 job roles enforced in Settings and Wizard
- [ ] Backend caps roles array at 10 items
- [ ] UI shows live `(X/10)` count
