Enforced a hard limit of 10 Job Roles across the Settings page, Setup Wizard, and backend settings storage, accompanied by an informative slot counter `(X/10)` and defensive input guardrails.

  - In `POST /api/settings`: Automatically cap `target_titles` to `slice(0, 10)` and `locations` to `slice(0, 5)` before persisting to settings and `master-profile.json`.
