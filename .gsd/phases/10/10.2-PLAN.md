# Plan 10.2: Frontend Dynamic Country-Aware Autocomplete & Location Suggestions

## Objective
Update the User Interface across both the Search Preferences (Settings) and the First-Time Setup Wizard (Step 2) so candidates can select any country configured in Settings (or Global Remote) and instantly get matching city autocomplete and tailored popular hub suggestion chips for that specific market.

## Context
- `public/index.html` (location inputs, datalists, quick suggestion chips in settings and wizard)
- `public/js/settings.js` (location tags, city autocomplete loading, country scope handler)
- `public/js/guide.js` (Setup Wizard Step 2 rendering and city chips)
- `public/data/global-cities.json` (multi-country city dataset)
