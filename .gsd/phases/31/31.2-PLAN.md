# Plan 31.2: Precision Role Relevance & Multi-Token Domain Boundary Filtering

## Objective
Prevent irrelevant, non-preference jobs from slipping into the scoring queue. Enforce strict multi-token domain boundaries and discipline ceilings in `isJobRoleRelevant()` so single-word matches (e.g. "power", "data") never match unrelated electrical/grid engineering ("Power Systems Senior Consultant / Power Grid SME"), cybersecurity compliance ("Senior Specialist, Data Loss Prevention"), or senior architectural roles ("Data Architect") unless the candidate explicitly targeted those disciplines.

## Context
- `scripts/core/geo-helper.js`
- `scripts/engine/fetch-all-boards.js`

## Tasks

<task type="auto">
  <name>Implement Multi-Token Domain Boundary & Unrelated Discipline Rejection</name>
  <files>scripts/core/geo-helper.js</files>
  <action>
    - In `scripts/core/geo-helper.js` inside `isJobRoleRelevant(jobTitle, targetTitles)`:
      1. **Multi-token compound protection**:
         - If target title has compound technical prefixes (e.g. "Power Developer", "Power BI Developer", "Power Platform Developer"):
           Do NOT treat isolated "power" as an arbitrary wildcard match for electrical grid, power utility, power generation, power systems, or power distribution jobs (e.g. reject "Power Systems Engineer", "Power Grid SME", "Substation Engineer").
         - If target title is "Data Analyst" or "BI Analyst":
           Do NOT treat "Data" as a wildcard for cybersecurity/infrastructure ("Data Loss Prevention", "DLP", "Data Center Facilities", "Data Cabling").
      2. **Discipline family boundary**:
         - Distinguish between Data Analytics/BI (Analyst, BI Developer, Analytics Engineer, Reporting Analyst) and:
           * Electrical/Power Grid Engineering
           * Cybersecurity / InfoSec (Data Loss Prevention, Threat Intelligence)
           * Enterprise Architecture / 10+ year Lead (Data Architect) unless candidate target titles include "architect" or candidate has 8+ years experience.
           * Data Science / Deep ML Modeling (Data Scientist, ML Researcher) unless candidate target titles include "scientist" or "machine learning".
      3. **Exact & multi-word priority**:
         - Always accept direct matches of candidate target titles.
         - For partial matches, require that the primary functional domain AND the role noun match within the same discipline family (e.g. "Pricing Analyst, Governance & Insights" is valid for Data Governance Analyst, but "Power Grid SME" is not valid for Power Developer).
  </action>
  <verify>node -e "const { isJobRoleRelevant } = require('./scripts/core/geo-helper'); console.log('Power Systems check:', isJobRoleRelevant('Power Systems Senior Consultant (Power Grid SME)', ['Power Developer', 'Data Analyst'])); console.log('Data Analyst check:', isJobRoleRelevant('Senior Business Intelligence Analyst', ['Data Analyst', 'BI Analyst']));"</verify>
  <done>Irrelevant roles like Power Grid SME and Data Loss Prevention are rejected, while genuine BI and Data Analyst opportunities pass cleanly.</done>
</task>
