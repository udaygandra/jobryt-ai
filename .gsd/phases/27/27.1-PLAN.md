# Plan 27.1: Full-Screen Blocking Loader with Jobryt AI Brand, Logo & Scanning Animation

## Objective
Provide an immersive, branded, full-screen blocking loader when a user uploads a resume from "My Resume & Profile". The loader features the Jobryt AI logo, website branding, animated orbital glow rings, and dynamic stage indicators to block interaction while communicating parse progress.

## Context
- `public/index.html`
- `public/css/components.css`
- `public/js/profile.js`

## Tasks

<task type="auto">
  <name>Build full-screen blocking loader overlay markup and animations</name>
  <files>public/index.html, public/css/components.css</files>
  <action>
    - In `public/index.html`:
      - Add `#resume-upload-blocking-overlay` with `fixed inset-0 z-50 flex items-center justify-center bg-black/85 backdrop-blur-md hidden transition-opacity duration-300`.
      - Include:
        * Jobryt gold logo badge (`.jobryt-logo-badge`) with pulse effect and rotating orbital glow ring.
        * Website branding: "Jobryt AI" with "Career Acceleration Engine" badge.
        * Animated scanning radar bar / pulse glow.
        * Dynamic status text container `#resume-upload-status-text` ("Extracting verifiable career history & skills...").
        * Subtle subtext: "Please wait while our engine analyzes your document and calibrates market target roles."
    - In `public/css/components.css`:
      - Add keyframes for orbital glow rotation (`@keyframes orbital-spin`), pulsing logo ring (`@keyframes logo-glow-pulse`), and indeterminate progress bar shimmer.

<task type="auto">
  <name>Wire full-screen loader lifecycle into profile resume upload</name>
  <files>public/js/profile.js</files>
  <action>
    - In `public/js/profile.js`:
      - Implement `showResumeUploadLoader()` and `hideResumeUploadLoader()`.
      - `showResumeUploadLoader()` activates the overlay, sets up step message interval ("Reading document structure..." ➔ "Extracting skills & career history..." ➔ "Discovering 4–10 market-validated target roles..." ➔ "Calibrating master profile...").
      - `hideResumeUploadLoader()` safely clears the interval and fades out the overlay.
      - Call `showResumeUploadLoader()` at the start of `handleResumeFileSelected` (when not inside the setup wizard).
      - Ensure `hideResumeUploadLoader()` is always called in `finally` or on error/success completion.
  </action>
  <verify>grep -n "showResumeUploadLoader" public/js/profile.js</verify>
  <done>Profile upload triggers the full-screen blocking loader on start and reliably dismisses on completion.</done>
</task>
