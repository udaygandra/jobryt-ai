# Plan 11.1: Premier Job Board-Grade City & Country Combobox Search (Settings UX)

## Objective
Upgrade the Settings page location selection experience from basic `<datalist>` to a job board-grade Combobox / Typeahead component (modeled after Google Jobs, LinkedIn, and Indeed). Provide fast, fuzzy multi-country city search, rich suggestion cards (city, state/province, country flag, tier badge), automatic country-city determination, smart scope alignment, keyboard navigation, and sleek removable location badges.
