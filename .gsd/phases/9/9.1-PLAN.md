# Plan 9.1: Multi-Preference Tagging, Canadian Cities Autocomplete, Application Status Pipeline & Scheduler Simplification

## Objective
Enable multi-role and multi-location search preferences with interactive badge tags, Canadian city autocompletion/validation, default workplace arrangement to "Open to All", ensure applying from Opportunities transitions applications to "Tailored (Ready to Submit)" rather than directly "Applied", simplify the scheduler UI for non-technical users, and remove the Telegram bot username header.

## Context
- Requirements 1, 2, 3, 8 from user prompt.
- `.gsd/SPEC.md`
- `public/index.html`
- `public/js/settings.js`
- `public/js/profile.js`
- `public/js/opportunities.js`
- `public/js/applied.js`
- `scripts/engine/tailor-engine.js`
- `src/routes/jobs.js`
