# Plan 17.4 Summary: Applications Page Card Optimization & Recruiter Intelligence

## Accomplishments
1. **Posting Link Fallback**:
   - For applications with direct links (`job_url` or `redirect_url`), renders `Job Link` button.
   - For applications lacking direct URLs, renders `Search Job` with a direct Google query (`https://www.google.com/search?q=${title + ' ' + company + ' job'}`), eliminating empty voids and allowing users to find the job posting with 1 click.

2. **In-Card Recruiter Finder Button**:
   - Added a styled, prominent `🤝 Find Recruiters` button on every application card action bar.
   - Fills empty horizontal whitespace and gives candidates instantaneous access to hiring intelligence.

3. **In-Card Recruiter Intelligence Drawer**:
   - Added `#recruiter-drawer-${app.id}` and `#recruiter-content-${app.id}` beneath each application card's notes drawer.
   - Fetches `/api/recruiters/search?company=${company}&city=${location}` with client-side caching.
   - Displays 1-click links for:
     - **Google X-Ray Talent/Recruiter Search**: Targets technical recruiters and talent acquisition on LinkedIn.
     - **Google X-Ray Leadership Search**: Targets engineering managers, directors, and department heads.
     - **LinkedIn People Search**: Direct link to company people search on LinkedIn.
     - **Tailored Outreach Note**: Pre-drafted LinkedIn connection request note (<= 300 characters) customized with role and company, with 1-click clipboard copy and toast notification.
