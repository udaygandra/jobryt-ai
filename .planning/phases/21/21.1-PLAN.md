# Plan 21.1: Pure #FFFFFF Backgrounds & Form Input Clarity for Resume, Profile & Settings in Light Theme

## Objective
Eliminate the gray background in light theme on all Resume and Profile input fields, textareas, selects, and tag containers that gave users the impression that fields were disabled or read-only. Enforce crisp, clean `#FFFFFF` backgrounds with clear `#CBD5E1` borders, distinct amber focus rings (`#D9A441`), and high-contrast dark text (`#1E232B`), ensuring fields look immediately interactive, welcoming, and editable.
