# Plan 32.1: Single-Step Tailored Package Modal (Cover Letter + Resume, "Download & Submit" / "Not Ready") with Deferred Commit

## Objective
Replace the two-step "Did you submit your application?" interstitial with ONE "Tailored Package" modal that shows the
cover letter and the attached tailored resume. The modal has two actions:

- **Download & Submit**: in one click, (1) opens the job posting in a new tab, (2) downloads the tailored PDF resume,
  (3) copies the cover letter to the clipboard and tells the user it was copied, and (4) moves the job from
  Opportunities to My Applications with stage **"Tailored (Ready to Submit)"** (`status = 'Tailored'`).
- **Not Ready**: closes the modal and leaves the job in Opportunities, unchanged. The modal's ✕ close button,
  Escape key and backdrop click do the same thing.

Today `/api/jobs/apply` writes to `applied_jobs` and deletes from `pending_jobs` as soon as tailoring finishes
(`tailor-engine.js` → `jobsDb.saveAppliedJob()` → `removePendingJob()`). So "Not Ready" can't work unless
committing is **deferred** until the user clicks Download & Submit.
