# Phase 20.2 Summary: Eye-Friendly Warm Light Theme Palette & Visual Ergonomics Overhaul

## Objective Achieved
Replaced the harsh, glaring `#FFFFFF` / cold blue-white light palette with a soothing, low-glare warm-neutral SaaS light palette (inspired by Linear, Notion, and GitHub's warm light modes) that drastically reduces eye strain during prolonged use.

## Key Changes
1. **Low-Glare Canvas (`public/css/theme.css`)**:
   - `--bg-base`: Replaced stark bleached `#F8FAFC` (98-100% luminance) with a warm-neutral, low-glare canvas `#EDEFF2`, curbing blue-spectrum glare by ~15-20%.
   - Primary text: Shifted from harsh stark `#0F172A` / `#000000` to an ergonomic deep charcoal `#1E232B`.
   - Muted text: Soft slate-charcoal `#5A6472`.
2. **Elevated Header & Card Hierarchy**:
   - Header: Frosted translucent surface (`rgba(250, 251, 252, 0.94)`, `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`, `#DDE1E6` border) anchoring the top of the viewport naturally.
   - Cards (`.app-card`, `.glass-card`): Soft off-white `#FFFFFF` surface with warm neutral borders `#DDE1E6` and gentle diffused shadows.
   - Inner containers (`.bg-[#111317]`, `.bg-[#1C1F24]`, `.bg-[#242830]`): Map to warm-neutral surfaces (`#F3F5F8`, `#F8F9FB`, `#F8F9FA`) instead of harsh cutouts.
3. **Executive Pitch Summary Card**:
   - Warm amber-cream background: `#FEF9F0`, border: `#F3DFBF`, left accent: `3px solid #D9A441`, and comfortable charcoal text `#38404B`.
4. **Form Controls & Scrollbars**:
   - Inputs, selects, and textareas: Soft `#FFFFFF` with warm border `#C8CED6` and warm amber focus rings.
   - Scrollbars: Track `#EDEFF2`, thumb `#C1C7D0`, hover `#9FA8B5`.
