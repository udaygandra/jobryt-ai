# Plan 15.2: Jargon Removal, Header/Navigation Breathing Room & Clean Layout Overhaul

## Objective
Eliminate dense technical jargon ("Zero n8n", "Ground Truth Policy", "Zero Hallucination", verbose card subtexts) across the app, slim down the top navigation bar to a modern compact height with breathing room, and improve component spacing so the user interface feels spacious, calm, and effortless to navigate.

## Context
- `public/index.html`
- `public/css/components.css`
- `public/css/style.css`

## Tasks

<task type="auto">
  <name>remove-technical-jargons</name>
  <files>public/index.html</files>
  <action>
    - Audit and replace all developer/technical jargon with clean, job-seeker-friendly copy:
      - Replace "100% Native Standalone • Zero n8n or external bot dependency" with "Fast & Direct • Scans job boards instantly".
      - Replace "Ground Truth Policy • Zero Hallucination • Strict verifiable profile alignment" with "Accurate Matching • Based strictly on your verified skills & experience".
      - Replace "Application Tracker • LinkedIn / Indeed Model • 2-step verification" with "Application Tracker • Organize and track your job applications".
      - Remove bloated 3-line repetitive descriptive paragraphs under page headers and form inputs; replace with concise 1-line guidance.
      - Clean up card subtitles in active boards and settings to be succinct and non-redundant.
  </action>
  <verify>grep -n "Zero n8n" public/index.html || true</verify>
  <done>Technical jargon removed; copy is natural, clean, and candidate-centric.</done>
</task>

<task type="auto">
  <name>compact-navbar-and-component-breathing-room</name>
  <files>public/index.html, public/css/components.css</files>
  <action>
    - Overhaul top desktop navigation bar:
      - Reduce height from bloated `py-5` (`h-20`) to modern, sleek `h-14`.
      - Optimize menu button sizes (`px-3 py-1.5 rounded-md text-xs`), ensuring clear visual hierarchy with compact count badges.
      - Group and space items gracefully so the navbar doesn't stretch or crowd the viewport edges.
    - Add component breathing room across view containers:
      - Add consistent `space-y-6` and `gap-6` across form sections.
      - Ensure headings, input containers, and buttons have comfortable margins and do not feel visually crammed.
  </action>
  <verify>grep -n "nav-tab" public/index.html</verify>
  <done>Top navigation is compact, polished, and all sections have generous breathing room.</done>
</task>

## Success Criteria
- [ ] No technical jargons ("Zero n8n", "Ground Truth Policy", etc.) visible in the UI
- [ ] Top navigation bar is compact, spacious, and responsive across viewports
- [ ] Forms and cards have comfortable spacing and clean hierarchy
