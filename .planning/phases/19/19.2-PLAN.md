# Plan 19.2: Complete UX Redesign of Application Cards & Space Utilization

## Objective
Redesign the application card in `public/js/applied.js` using a structured 3-tier layout (Header with metrics & stage, full-width executive pitch body, and bottom action toolbar). Eliminate the massive empty void between text and floated buttons, drastically reduce vertical height, and achieve an intuitive, premium SaaS aesthetic.

## Context
- `public/js/applied.js`
- User feedback and screenshot showing disconnected left text and far-right floated buttons with a large empty gap in between.
