# Plan 13.3: Resume-Driven Automatic Country Detection & Zero-Friction Scope Unlocking

## Objective
Eliminate the disabled city input trap when a country is detected from an uploaded resume or master profile. Automatically detect candidate country during resume extraction, synchronize `#cfg-scope` in Settings, and trigger `onCountryScopeChange()` so the city combobox is seamlessly unlocked, enabled, and populated with that country's suggestions without requiring manual user re-selection.
